# Regulatory Acceptance & Compliance Standards

## Are Digital Temperature Logs Accepted by Food Inspectors?

**Yes, when they meet Food Safety Program requirements.**

Digital temperature monitoring systems are accepted by regulators across Australia under FSANZ Standard 3.2.2 (Food Safety Practices) and Standard 3.2.3 (Food Premises and Equipment), **provided they comply with your approved Food Safety Program**.

## What Your Food Safety Program Must Include

Under FSANZ Standard 3.2.1, your Food Safety Program must:
1. Identify food safety hazards
2. Identify Critical Control Points (CCPs)
3. Establish monitoring procedures
4. Establish corrective actions when CCPs fail
5. Establish verification procedures
6. Document all of the above

**How Cured Compliance supports this**:
- **Identifies hazards**: Pre-loaded FSANZ standards, HACCP templates
- **Monitors CCPs**: Automated temperature logging for cold chain, cooking logs for RTE products
- **Corrective actions**: System prompts staff to document actions when temps breach
- **Verification**: Audit reports, compliance dashboards, trend analysis
- **Documentation**: All records stored permanently with timestamps and photo evidence

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can inspectors access my data during an audit?**  
A: Yes, instantly. Log in on any device and show live or historical data. Or print PDF reports. You control access—inspectors don't get permanent logins unless you choose to provide one.

**Q: What if my internet is down during an audit?**  
A: Sensors continue logging locally (data stored on gateway). Data uploads automatically when internet reconnects. If internet is down during the audit, you can still show historical data from before the outage (already uploaded to cloud).

**Q: How long do you retain records?**  
A: 5+ years minimum (exceeds regulatory requirements of 2-3 years). Records never expire unless you manually delete them.

**Q: Are digital signatures legally acceptable?**  
A: Yes. Australian law recognizes digital signatures as legally binding under the Electronic Transactions Act 1999 (Cth). Food inspectors accept digital signatures on checklists and forms.

**Q: What if I switch back to paper later?**  
A: You can export all your historical data as PDFs anytime. But once customers see how much time digital saves, they never go back to paper.

**Q: Does Cured Compliance guarantee I'll pass my audit?**  
A: No software can guarantee audit outcomes—that depends on your actual food safety practices. What Cured Compliance guarantees: accurate documentation of what you're actually doing, making it easy to prove compliance to inspectors.
