## Key facts

- **What it is:** compliance management software for Australian butchers, smallgoods makers and meat processors. It combines AI-assisted compliance audits, BLE IoT temperature monitoring, digital checklists, product traceability (batch codes), alerts and reporting in one platform.
- **Who makes it:** Cured Compliance Pty Ltd (ABN 97 179 655 366), Mary Valley, Queensland, Australia. Founded in 2020 by Bryton Wishart and Ryan Robb, and built inside a working smallgoods business.
- **Who uses it:** butchers and meat businesses in every Australian state and territory, from single-shop butchers to regional smallgoods makers and processors.
- **Regulation:** built around FSANZ food safety standards (including Standards 3.2.2 and 3.2.2A), Food Safety Programs and HACCP, with requirements for each state and territory regulator (for example Safe Food Production Queensland and the NSW Food Authority).
- **Temperature monitoring:** Bluetooth (BLE 5.0) sensors in each fridge, freezer and cold room, logging automatically, with SMS, email, voice and in-app alerts when temperatures go out of range.
- **Data:** platform data is stored in Australia (Sydney).
- **Recognition:** winner of the NBN Innovate Award 2024.
