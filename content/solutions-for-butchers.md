## The Butcher's Compliance Challenge

Running a butcher shop or meat processing facility means juggling:
- Daily temperature checks (fridges, cool rooms, freezers)
- Pre-start cleaning checklists
- HACCP documentation

- Batch traceability from kill to consumer

**Traditional paper-based systems take 2+ days per month in manual admin.**

### Dry Ager Monitoring
**Scenario**: Premium butcher shop ages beef for 28-45 days.

**With Cured Compliance**:
- V3 Temp & Humidity sensor in dry ager cabinet
- Tracks temperature (1-3°C) AND humidity (70-85%)
- Logs every 5 minutes for precise aging control
- SMS alert if humidity drops (prevents case-hardening)
- Generates aging reports for customers ("Your steak aged 42 days at 2°C, 78% humidity")

## Common Butcher Questions Answered

**"Do I need this if I'm just a small single-operator shop in a rural town?"**  
Yes. Even sole traders face audits, and one failed fridge overnight can cost $15,000+ in lost stock. Our smallest customers are family-run shops with 2-3 staff in regional Australia.

**"Will my staff actually use it?"**  
Yes. Tablets are easier than clipboards, photo uploads prevent disputes ("I did clean that!"), and digital signatures are faster than pen-and-paper. Staff prefer it after the first week.

**"What if I already have Clever Logger or other temp sensors?"**  
Cured Compliance is a standalone system that replaces existing temp loggers. Install our sensors alongside your current ones for 24 hours to compare, then remove the old system. Our sensors last longer (2+ years battery) and include compliance checklists, traceability, and audit tools all integrated.

**"How long does setup take?"**  
30 minutes per staff member. We configure your fridges, create user logins, assign roles, and train your team on tablets. You're operational by lunch.

**"What if my internet goes down?"**  
Sensors continue logging locally (stored on gateway). Data uploads when internet reconnects. Real-time alerts pause during outage but resume when back online.
