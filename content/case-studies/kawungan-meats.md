Dale Newberry is a fifth-generation butcher and the owner of Kawungan Quality Meats, supplying wholesale and retail customers right across Hervey Bay.

## The challenge

Before Cured Compliance, Dale ran his food safety program with pen and paper, using the diaries provided through AMIC. The hardest part wasn't the paperwork itself; it was staying on top of it:

- Getting every record done **on time, all the time**
- Working back through the book to make sure everything was done the way the auditors wanted
- Building a system he thought was right, only to find the goalposts had shifted by the next audit

## What they did

Kawungan started with the everyday essentials in Cured Compliance:

- Daily reporting
- Maintenance checklists
- Management reviews

Once those were running smoothly, they moved into **batch coding**. Today every ham, roast beef and cabana that leaves the shop carries a batch code, giving Kawungan full product traceability.

## The results

- **Everything in one place.** Recording, documenting and auditing all live in a single system.
- **Full traceability** on every product that leaves the shop.
- **Auditors were impressed.** They were surprised to see a shop of Kawungan's size working to the same standard as the big operators.
- **Confidence on audit day.** When the auditors walk in, Dale knows the shop is exactly where it needs to be.

> "I would never go back to pen and paper. It's a phenomenal system." — Dale Newberry, Owner, Kawungan Quality Meats
